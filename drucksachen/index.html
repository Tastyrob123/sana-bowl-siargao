SanaBowl Superfood Café · General Luna, Siargao

Every piecefor the café.

The website, eight tools and 32 pieces for print and Instagram, in the order of a day at the café on Tourism Road, from 7 AM to 11 PM.

From 7 AMThe stickers

7 AM · On the table

Stands, napkins, coasters

Four inserts for the acrylic stand, each with front and back: the review stand in violet and sky blue, the add-ons with prices and two of the café’s lines. Three napkins, two coasters and a placemat with the six smoothie bowls.

On the tableStickers

10 AM · To go

Lids, sleeves and bags

Lid labels to tick at the counter, built like the menu board with base, toppings and add-ons, three sleeves for the coffee cup, two paper bags, the till receipt, flags for the bowls to go and two stamps.

To goStickers

12 PM · The menu

Five boards in print

Five pages like the five boards in the café, from the same file as the website. Violet like the boards or light with photos, each in A4 and A3.

The menuStickers

3 PM · On Tourism Road

For hostels and surf camps

Flyers, postcards and a poster with the map of General Luna, Tourism Road and SanaBowl on it, plus an A-board and a banner for the road.

The flyers

6 PM · At the mural

Thirty-seven stickers

Thirty-seven stickers on five sheets: the sign, the mural, the café’s own lines and the six smoothie bowls, each sheet as a print file with cut line and as SVG.

The stickers

9 PM · On Instagram

The next sixty posts

The next sixty posts in a phone you can scroll: each smoothie bowl with its base and toppings, poke and rice bowls, toasts, coffee and shakes, the menu boards, the terrace and the guests at the mural. Plus seventeen stories for the bowls and the café’s lines, and covers for the two highlights.

The feedThe stories

And online · SanaBowl on the web

Website, tools and the proposal

Three pages for the phone and the computer: Home, Menu and Visit. Eight tools in the look of the café: Control panel, Edit website, Menus & price lists, Inquiries & chat, Visitors, Brand book, Sticker editor, Print & feed. A film of the website, and the proposal as a PDF.
