Superfood café on Tourism Road

SanaBowl is a women-owned café in General Luna, Siargao. Outside, wooden benches with purple cushions stand under the thatched roof, inside there is a painted mural and Wi-Fi.

Eat in, take away or order delivery.

Base and toppings

Six smoothie bowls. Each base is blended with vegan milk, the toppings go on top. Organic Açai is made with authentic Brazilian organic açai.

Add-ons from ₱40: peanut butter, almonds, walnuts, cacao nibs, dates, vanilla whey or vegan protein, creatine, collagen.
